# Deepcore

The dropship is scrap. The rescue channel is dead. Beneath your boots lies a planet full of valuable ore—and the parts you need to build your own way home. **Deepcore** is a solo subterranean mining expedition where every descent is a wager on the climb back.

Brace your drill, carve a shaft, and follow bright mineral seams through the rock. Fill your cargo bay, then fire the jetpack into the darkness above. Weight changes everything: a rich haul climbs slower, burns more fuel, and can become too heavy to lift. Sometimes the smartest thing you can do is leave a fortune behind.

Back at the surface camp, sell the haul and decide what matters most: a full tank, a stronger drill, a tougher suit, or another piece of the escape rocket. Seven upgrade tracks and six field supplies let you prepare for longer trips. Hunt the buried Resonite and Cryenite with a scanner, watch for the faint breath of gas, and pick your way around molten pools.

At the bottom, the Core waits. Its Sample gives you ninety seconds to reach the launch pad and finish the ignition system. Choose Quick, Standard, or Marathon depths; save your progress at camp in Standard mode, or stake the entire expedition on Hardcore. There is only one way to win: build the rocket, and leave.
